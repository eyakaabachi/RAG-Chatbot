GENERAL TERMS

Section 1
This insurance policy covers material and bodily damage sustained by the
policyholder under the conditions described below. This document is a
synthetic example and does not constitute a real contract.

Section 2
The deductible applicable in the event of a claim is set at 300 dollars
for material damage and 600 dollars for bodily injury. This deductible
remains the policyholder's responsibility and is subtracted from the
compensation amount.

Section 3
In the event of a claim, the policyholder has seven business days to
report the claim to the insurer, starting from the date they became aware
of it. After this deadline, the insurer may deny coverage.

Section 4
Coverage applies to the following events: fire, water damage, burglary,
and any natural disaster officially recognized by the relevant authority.
Damage resulting from clear lack of maintenance is excluded.

Section 5
Termination of the contract may be initiated by either the policyholder or
the insurer, with two months notice before the contract's annual renewal
date.

Section 6
Compensation is paid within a maximum of thirty days from the date both
parties agree on the claim amount, provided the policyholder has submitted
all requested supporting documents.
